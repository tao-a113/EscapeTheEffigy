Evacuation Procedures

Are you currently in a life-threatening emergency situation? Do you need to evacuate the ship? Not to worry! This ship is fitted with a regulation compliant HadronARK© emergency shuttle. In the case of a catastrophic event please follow the standard evacuation procedure:

Divert power to the shuttle
Ensure food, medical supplies and oxygen are transferred to the shuttle
Open the shuttle bay doors
Board the shuttle
Input the shuttle launch codes
Take off and head to safety!

We hope you have a pleasant emergency!
