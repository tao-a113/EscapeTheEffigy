Welcome to the escape the Effigy escape room

follow these steps to get set up

1. Go to https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wMQ0AqyFEEXh5dzuNsTnwxpHBdUoYZ6S?usp=sharing

2. Right click the folder called "Escape Room (download me)" and download it

3. Find the downloaded zip folder in your downloads directory

4. Right click the folder and choose "extract all" option. 

close all chrome tabs and windows before you do step 5 and 6. This is very important.

5. Go into the new folder that you have just extracted. Then click the "run escape room in chrome.bat" file

6. An error should appear. That's fine, click on the "more info" option and then click "run anyway"
