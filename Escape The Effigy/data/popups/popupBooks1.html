NAME OF SPACESHIPAriboo 3
TYPE OF SPACESHIPPersonal explorer / luxury transport
SYSTEM OF MANUFACTUREGolk
COST WHEN NEW$42,000,000
NUMBER OF CREW20
MAXIMUM SPEED355 LYPD
These spacecraft are some of the most incredible vehicles available and yet are only available to citizens of the wealthiest system: Golk.They represent the ultimate in materialistic excess today. Golk’s unparalleled productivity is due to vast cybernetic systems which churn out machinery and goods which are at the cutting edge of hi-technology. The Ariboo 3 is an enormously popular ‘personal spacecraft’ of enormous size but fits the category only due to it’s usage. It is often heavily customized to taste and outfitted to sumptuous levels.
NAME OF SPACESHIPAyail
TYPE OF SPACESHIPLarge Freighter
SYSTEM OF MANUFACTUREDilcom
COST WHEN NEW$156,000,000
NUMBER OF CREW42
MAXIMUM SPEED150 LYPD
A very large freighter, the Ayail class is designed to carry enormous containers that hold an enormous open volume of just about anything that needs to be transported around the galaxy - even other spacecraft! These craft are amongst the biggest civilian spaceships that have ever been made and are a rare sight indeed with just 32 being made. Made in theDilcom system which is one of the newer sectors of space they have contributed much to the economy of the new colony world.
NAME OF SPACESHIPOORSTANISCLAV
TYPE OF SPACESHIPCorporate Ship
SYSTEM OF MANUFACTUREYark
COST WHEN NEW$1,200,000,000
NUMBER OF CREW3,500
MAXIMUM SPEED20 LYPD
Oorstanisclav is named after the ruler of the Yark Empire who lived for 420 years after inheriting the fledgling colony world from his father Oorstanisclav. It is unparalleled in its combination of size and wealth it creates. There are other corporate ships but none like this incredible vessel, as this ship generates many billions of galactic dollars per week as it traverses the galaxy. It’s ability to absorb many large freighters and loads of raw minerals daily is simply awe inspiring.
NAME OF SPACESHIPPrastus
TYPE OF SPACESHIPBattleship
SYSTEM OF MANUFACTUREBrak 2
COST WHEN NEW$450,000,000
NUMBER OF CREW5,000
MAXIMUM SPEED650 LYPD
This vast class of battleship has patrolled the Brak 2 system for over a hundred years and rather successfully, no raid on the wealthy homeland of the Brak species has ever been successful. The Prastus class remains one of the most capable military ships ever built, but little detail is known about the armaments that she contains. It is known that they house several thousand fighters which are incredibly fast, maneuverable and armed with a powerful anti-matter cannon.
